# Widget ideas, deduplicated (9 November 2026)

Deduplicated from the 285-entry macOS widget preview catalog: one entry per
thing a widget does, with every catalog entry it absorbed listed under
"from". Widget suites and frameworks are gone (they are collections, not
widgets); the suites whose widgets the catalog listed one by one are covered
through those. Styles of the same widget (analog, flip, word clock) are one
widget with options.

**Exists** marks what neoshell already has, and where.

## Time & date (11)

- **Clock**: the time, in a digital, analog, flip or word style. *Exists in the bar; no desktop card yet.* From: Apple Clock, MD Clock, Desktop Flip Clock, Widgetscape Time, Widgetscape Analog Clock, BetterMacWidgets Clock / Flip Clock / Word Clock / Simple Clock, WidgetScreen Clock.
- **World clock**: several time zones at once. From: Time Zone Pro, World Clock Widgets, Widgetscape World Clock, BetterMacWidgets World Clock, Übersicht World Clock Widget.
- **Countdown**: days until a date. From: Bears Countdown, Widgetscape Countdown, BetterMacWidgets Countdown, WidgetScreen Countdown, Übersicht Countdown.
- **Timer**: a visual countdown of minutes. From: Time's Up!, BetterMacWidgets Timer.
- **Stopwatch**. From: BetterMacWidgets Stopwatch, WidgetScreen Stopwatch.
- **Pomodoro**: focus and break intervals. *Exists as a status item.* From: Widgetscape Focus Timer, BetterMacWidgets Pomodoro, WidgetScreen Pomodoro, Übersicht Timer Widget.
- **Time progress**: how much of the day, week or year has passed. From: Widgetscape Day Dial, Widgetscape Week Progress, BetterMacWidgets Year Progress / Day Progress, WidgetScreen Day Bar.
- **Month calendar**: the month at a glance. From: Mini Calendar Widget, Widgetscape Calendar, WidgetScreen Calendar, WidgetScreen Month Dots.
- **Agenda / up next**: today's and upcoming events. *Exists as the Agenda status item and the Nook calendar; no desktop card yet.* From: Apple Calendar, Fantastical, BusyCal, Agenda, Widgetscape Agenda, BetterMacWidgets Calendar, WidgetScreen Next Event, Übersicht Berries Calendar, Google Calendar widget, Google Calendar Upcoming Events.
- **Day timeline**: today's events laid out on a time axis. From: Structured, Widgetscape Day Timeline.
- **Prayer times**. From: Prayer Times Mac.

## Tasks & notes (9)

- **Tasks**: due and open to-dos from one or more sources (local, Todoist, Google Tasks, Taskwarrior), as a list or a board. *Exists as local tasks in the Agenda status item.* From: Apple Reminders, Things 3, OmniFocus 4, TickTick, GoodTask, 2Do, Smart Tasks, Kanban – Doneit, Widgetscape Reminders, BetterMacWidgets Reminders, WidgetScreen Reminders, Übersicht Berries Reminders, Google Tasks, Taskwarrior Widget, Inbox for Todoist.
- **Habits**: daily habits and streaks. From: Streaks, Awesome Habits, Widgetscape Habit Streak, Übersicht Berries Streaks (TickTick's habits too).
- **Focus goal**: one line, the thing to do today. From: One Goal, One Thing.
- **Notes**: sticky notes, pinned and recent notes, voice notes. From: Apple Notes, Bear, Noted, Memo Widget, Stickies, Dash Dot Dash, Widgetscape Sticky Notes, BetterMacWidgets Sticky Note, WidgetScreen Sticky Note.
- **Journal**: today's prompt and recent entries. From: Day One.
- **Contacts**: favourite people and quick actions. From: Cardhop.
- **Time tracking**: running timer and timesheet. From: Tyme.
- **Timetable**: classes and homework. From: SchoolOrganizer.
- **Notion database**: items from a Notion database. From: NotionistWidget.
